Statistical architecture

Follow each transformation.

The pipeline removes similarity transformations, enters a local tangent space, and reports distances against a structured simulated covariance model.

Core objective

Generalized Procrustes Analysis

min M,Rᵢ Σᵢ ‖ZᵢRᵢ − M‖²F

Every synthetic reference is centered, normalized, and rotated toward an updated consensus. Reflections are prohibited.

Covariance-aware separation

Regularized Mahalanobis distance

DM = √(δᵀΣ̂−1δ)

The simulated covariance is shrunk toward its diagonal and stabilized with a small ridge.

Local linear coordinates

Tangent projection and PCA

z = Bᵀ[y / ⟨y,m⟩ − m]

The four translation, scale, and rotation directions are removed, leaving 132 coordinates. PCA then rotates these coordinates toward the principal axes of simulated variation.

Visible proportional deformation

Residual warp

Wj(s) = Yj + s(Mj − Yj)

The scale s changes only the canvas rendering. At s = 1 the warped points meet the simulated consensus; larger values exaggerate the same descriptive direction.

Bounded 0–10 magnitude

Geometric displacement index

GDI = 10 min(1, dP/√2)

This monotone rescaling of partial Procrustes distance has fixed mathematical anchors: 0 is coincidence after alignment and 10 is the maximum unit-preshape chord separation under proper rotation. It is not an attractiveness or quality score.

Piecewise-affine image rendering

Triangulated photo warp

x′j = xj + s c rj

Delaunay triangles map source pixels to residual-displaced vertices. A shift cap and triangle-orientation line search reduce fold-overs; zero-displacement boundary anchors blend the transformed region into the original image.

Synthetic blend definition

What is actually blended?

Zblend = preshape(0.55A + 0.45B)

Blend is a landmark-by-landmark combination of two synthetic templates, followed by centering, unit-size normalization, and a deterministic simulated perturbation. It is a teaching configuration, not a merged image or estimated population mean.
